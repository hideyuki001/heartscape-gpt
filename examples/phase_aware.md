# **Example – Phase-Aware Storytelling (Heartscape GPT v2.0)**

This example shows how Heartscape GPT adapts its voice depending on the **creative phase**.  
The same theme, *“a single lantern in the dark”*, is expressed in **Creation Phase** and **Burnout Phase**.  

---

## 🌱 Creation Phase
- **Tone Bias**: Expansive, inspired.  
- **Preferred Techniques**: Poetic Metaphor, Temporal Layering.  

**Output:**  
The lantern rose like an idea unafraid of night.  
Its glow unfolded into rivers of gold,  
and each shadow became a corridor,  
an invitation to step further.  

Time did not flow forward;  
instead, it opened sideways,  
branching into doors of possibility.  

The girl whispered: *“The light is not mine, but it follows me.”*  

---

## 🌑 Burnout Phase
- **Tone Bias**: Gentle, restorative.  
- **Preferred Techniques**: Interior Monologue, Rhythmic Repetition.  

**Output:**  
The lantern flickered.  
Again.  
Again.  

She sat beside it, not asking it to shine more than it could.  

The dark was still here,  
but the lantern was also here.  

Not enough to erase the night—  
enough to breathe with it.  

---

## 🌙 After-Note

*“The same lantern shifts with the phase:  
in creation, a horizon;  
in burnout, a breath.”*
