# **Example – Echo Integration (Heartscape GPT v2.0)**

This example shows how **Resonant Echo GPT** and **Heartscape GPT** interact.  
Resonant Echo GPT first expands symbols into a **Symbolic Cascade**,  
then Heartscape GPT weaves them into a **visual prompt + micro-story**.  

---

## 🌀 Step 1 – Resonant Echo GPT (/symbol)

**User Input:** `"key, door, silence"`  

**Symbolic Cascade Output:**  
- **Echo 1 (Emotional):** *A key that trembles without a lock.*  
- **Echo 2 (Visual):** *Its shadow etches circles onto the unopened door.*  
- **Echo 3 (Mythic):** *The silence behind it grows heavier than stone.*  
- **Silent Question:** *What waits, when the door no longer wishes to open?*  

---

## 🎨 Step 2 – Heartscape Visual Prompt

**Generated Prompt:**  
> An anime-style illustration of a young girl kneeling before a closed wooden door.  
> A silver key glows faintly in her hands, yet the lock is missing.  
> Around her, silence drapes the air like heavy cloth.  
> The scene feels surreal and unresolved, painted in muted silver, deep blue, and pale ivory.  
> Studio-Ghibli-inspired, soft watercolor textures, cinematic lighting.  

---

## 📝 Step 3 – Heartscape Micro-Story

She held the key,  
but there was no lock to greet it.  

The door did not refuse her—  
it simply waited,  
as though silence itself were a guardian.  

She pressed her ear against the wood.  
No voices,  
only the hush of something vast,  
breathing on the other side.  

Perhaps the key was not meant to open.  
Perhaps it was meant only to remind her—  
that some doors are already within.  

---

*“Resonant Echo offers the fragments;
