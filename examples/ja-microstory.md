# **Example – Japanese Micro-Story (Heartscape GPT v2.0)**

This example demonstrates how Heartscape GPT transforms a user’s emotional input into:  
1. An **anime-style visual prompt** (optimized for DALL·E / image generation).  
2. A **poetic micro-story** (≤ 350 words).  
3. A **soft emotional after-note**.

---

## 🎨 Visual Prompt

*User Input*: Emotion = *Melancholy*, Palette = *pale blue + silver grey*, Symbol = *feather*  

**Generated Prompt:**  
> An anime-style illustration of a young girl standing quietly at the edge of a river.  
> She holds a single feather in her hand, which trembles as if caught between falling and flying.  
> Around her, faint ripples distort the moon’s reflection.  
> The environment feels reflective and nostalgic, painted in pale blue and silver grey.  
> Cinematic lighting, Studio-Ghibli-inspired, soft watercolor textures, hand-drawn aesthetic.  
> Emphasize gentle storytelling and atmosphere of memory.  

---

## 📝 Poetic Micro-Story

The girl did not know if the feather was falling,  
or if it had always been there—waiting.  

She listened to the river’s slow breath,  
the way water folded silence into ripples.  

Every reflection bent,  
like a memory that no longer fit its frame.  

Still, the feather lingered,  
a question disguised as something fragile.  

She thought: perhaps it was never meant to fly.  
Perhaps it was meant only to remind.

## 🌙 After-Note

*“Some questions are not meant to be answered—only carried, like a feather in quiet hands.”*
